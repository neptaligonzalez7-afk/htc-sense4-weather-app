## HTC Sense 4 Weather — v0.2.2 (Nov 2026)

**First public release.** The HTC One X (Sense 4) Weather app rebuilt for modern Android, with Open-Meteo weather and the Sense 4 home-screen widgets.

### Highlights

- **Animated weather scenes** from the One X: sky backdrops and condition animations for day and night, cross-fading when the weather changes.
- **Now / Hourly / 4-day tabs**, plus feels like, humidity, wind, visibility, sunrise and sunset.
- **Up to 15 cities**: swipe up and down between them, search to add, find your current location, reorder or delete in Edit list.
- **°F / °C**, with miles or kilometres to match.
- **Sense 4 widgets**: Weather (three sizes), Clock, World clock, Dual clock, Digital clock, Flip clock (small), Flip clock + headlines, Ring clock and Spin clock.
- **Works with the [HTC Weather Flip Clock](https://github.com/neptaligonzalez7-afk/htc-weather-flip-clock)** v1.2.11+: tapping the flip clock's weather opens this app.

### Requirements

Android 12 or newer (API 31+), internet for weather. Location permission is optional.
